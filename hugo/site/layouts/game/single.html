How Anthem Battle works
  • Listen first: You must listen to each anthem for at least 3 seconds before your vote counts.
  • Vote: Click "Vote for this anthem" to declare a winner. The other anthem gets a loss.
  • ELO ratings: Wins and losses update each country's ELO score (starting at 1500, K=32). A win against a higher-rated country earns more points.
  • Wildcard: Occasionally a lower-ranked anthem is matched against a higher-ranked one — upsets are worth more.
  • Skip: Not feeling it? Skip the matchup and get a fresh pair.
