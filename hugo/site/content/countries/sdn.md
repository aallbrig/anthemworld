**Sudan** is a country in Northern Africa, Africa. Its capital is Khartoum. The national anthem is *Nahnu Jund Allah Jund Al-watan* ("We Are the Army of God and of Our Land").

## National Anthem

**English title:** We Are the Army of God and of Our Land

**Composer:** Ahmed Morjan

**Lyricist:** Ahmed Mohamed Saleh

adopted 1956; originally served as the anthem of the Sudanese military
