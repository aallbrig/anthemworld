**Mali** is a country in Western Africa, Africa. Its capital is Bamako. The national anthem is *Pour l'Afrique et pour toi* ("Mali").

## National Anthem

**English title:** Mali

**Composer:** Banzumana Sissoko

**Lyricist:** Seydou Badian Kouyatén

**Adopted:** 1962
