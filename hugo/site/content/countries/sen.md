**Senegal** is a country in Western Africa, Africa. Its capital is Dakar. The national anthem is *Le Lion rouge* ("Onward, Singapore").

## National Anthem

**English title:** Onward, Singapore

**Composer:** Herbert Pepper

**Lyricist:** Léopold Sédar Senghor

adopted 1959; the anthem is sung only in Malay; first four lines of the melody are used as a presidential salute
