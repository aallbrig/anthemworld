**葡萄牙** 是一个国家，位于 Southern Europe, Europe，首都是 Lisbon，国歌是 *A Portuguesa* ("The Song of the Portuguese")。

## 国歌

**英文名称：** The Song of the Portuguese

**作曲：** Alfredo Keil

**作词：** Henrique Lopes de Mendonça

adopted 1911; originally written to protest the Portuguese monarchy's acquiescence to the 1890 British ultimatum forcing Portugal to give up areas of Africa
