**South Africa** es un país en Southern Africa, Africa. Su capital es Pretoria. El himno nacional es *national anthem of South Africa* ("Stand and Sing of Zambia, Proud and Free").

## Himno Nacional

**Título en inglés:** Stand and Sing of Zambia, Proud and Free

**Compositor:** Enoch Mankayi Sontonga

**Letrista:** Enoch Mankayi Sontonga

adopted 1964; the melody, which comes from the popular song "God Bless Africa," a popular song and anthem in southern Africa
