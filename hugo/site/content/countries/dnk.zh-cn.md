**丹麦** 是一个国家，位于 Northern Europe, Europe，首都是 Copenhagen，国歌是 *Kong Christian stod ved højen mast* ("King Christian Stood by the Lofty Mast")。

## 国歌

**英文名称：** King Christian Stood by the Lofty Mast

**作曲：** Ditlev Ludvig Rogert

**作词：** Johannes Ewald

adopted 1780; one of the oldest royal anthems in the world; used for events when Danish royalty is present; anthem has equal status with the national anthem
