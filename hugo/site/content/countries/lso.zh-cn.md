**莱索托** 是一个国家，位于 Southern Africa, Africa，首都是 Maseru，国歌是 *Lesotho Fatse La Bontata Rona* ("High Above the Young Rhine")。

## 国歌

**英文名称：** High Above the Young Rhine

**作曲：** Ferdinand Samuel Laur

**作词：** Joshua Pulomo Mohapeloa

adopted 1850, revised 1963; uses the tune of the United Kingdom's anthem, "God Save the King"
