**Thailand** is a country in South-Eastern Asia, Asia. Its capital is Bangkok. The national anthem is *Thai National Anthem* ("A Salute to the Monarch").

## National Anthem

**English title:** A Salute to the Monarch

**Composer:** Phra Chenduriyang (Piti Wathayakon)

**Lyricist:** Luang Saranupraphan

royal anthem, played in the presence of the royal family and during certain state ceremonies
