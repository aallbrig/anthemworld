**Cameroon** is a country in Middle Africa, Africa. Its capital is Yaoundé. The national anthem is *O Cameroon, Cradle of Our Forefathers*.

## National Anthem

**Composer:** René Jam Afane

**Lyricist:** Samuel Minkyo

adopted 1957; lyrics were changed slightly to the current version in 1978
