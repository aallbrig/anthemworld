**Zimbabwe** es un país en Southern Africa, Africa. Su capital es Harare. El himno nacional es *National Anthem of Zimbabwe* ("Blessed Be the Land of Zimbabwe").

## Himno Nacional

**Título en inglés:** Blessed Be the Land of Zimbabwe

**Letrista:** Solomon Mutswairo

adopted 1994; lyrics in the country's three main languages were written by Zimbabwean poet and academic MUTSWAIRO
