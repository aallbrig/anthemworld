**老挝** 是一个国家，位于 South-Eastern Asia, Asia，首都是 Vientiane，国歌是 *Pheng Xat Lao* ("Hymn of the Lao People")。

## 国歌

**英文名称：** Hymn of the Lao People

**作曲：** Thongdy Sounthone Vichit

**作词：** Sisana Sisane

music adopted 1945, lyrics adopted 1975; the anthem's lyrics were changed after the communist revolution that overthrew the monarchy in 1975
