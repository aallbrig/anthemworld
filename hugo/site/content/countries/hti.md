**Haiti** is a country in Caribbean, Americas. Its capital is Port-au-Prince. The national anthem is *La Dessalinienne* ("The Dessalines Song").

## National Anthem

**English title:** The Dessalines Song

**Composer:** Nicolas Geffrard

**Lyricist:** Justin Lhérisson

adopted 1904; named for Jean-Jacques DESSALINES, founder of Haiti
