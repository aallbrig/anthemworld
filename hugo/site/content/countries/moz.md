**Mozambique** is a country in Eastern Africa, Africa. Its capital is Maputo. The national anthem is *Pátria Amada* ("Lovely Fatherland").

## National Anthem

**English title:** Lovely Fatherland

**Composer:** Salomão J. Manhiça

**Lyricist:** Salomão J. Manhiça

adopted 2002; the new anthem reflects the new multi-party political system
