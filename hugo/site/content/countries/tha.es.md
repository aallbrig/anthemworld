**Thailand** es un país en South-Eastern Asia, Asia. Su capital es Bangkok. El himno nacional es *Thai National Anthem* ("A Salute to the Monarch").

## Himno Nacional

**Título en inglés:** A Salute to the Monarch

**Compositor:** Phra Chenduriyang (Piti Wathayakon)

**Letrista:** Luang Saranupraphan

royal anthem, played in the presence of the royal family and during certain state ceremonies
