**Ireland** is a country in Northern Europe, Europe. Its capital is Dublin. The national anthem is *Amhrán na bhFiann* ("The Soldier's Song").

## National Anthem

**English title:** The Soldier's Song

**Composer:** Patrick Heeney

**Lyricist:** Peadar Kearney

adopted 1926; the song "Ireland's Call" is often used as the anthem at athletic events if citizens of Ireland and Northern Ireland are competing as a unified team
