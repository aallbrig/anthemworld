**Vietnam** is a country in South-Eastern Asia, Asia. Its capital is Hanoi. The national anthem is *Tiến Quân Ca* ("The Song of the Marching Troops").

## National Anthem

**English title:** The Song of the Marching Troops

**Composer:** Văn Cao

**Lyricist:** Văn Cao

**Adopted:** 1944-01-01

adopted as the national anthem of the Democratic Republic of Vietnam in 1945; it became the national anthem of the unified Socialist Republic of Vietnam in 1976; only the first verse is used as the official anthem
