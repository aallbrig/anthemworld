**France** es un país en Western Europe, Europe. Su capital es Paris. El himno nacional es *La Marseillaise* ("The Song of Marseille").

## Himno Nacional

**Título en inglés:** The Song of Marseille

**Compositor:** Claude Joseph Rouget de Lisle

**Letrista:** Claude Joseph Rouget de Lisle

**Adoptado:** 1792-05-06

adopted 1795, restored 1870; acquired its name when the National Guard of Marseille sang the song while marching into Paris in 1792 during the French Revolution; one of the most recognized anthems in the world
