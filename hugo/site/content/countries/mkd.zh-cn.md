**北馬其頓** 是一个国家，位于 Southeast Europe, Europe，首都是 Skopje，国歌是 *Denes nad Makedonija* ("Today Over Macedonia")。

## 国歌

**英文名称：** Today Over Macedonia

**作曲：** Todor Skalovski

**作词：** Vlado Maleski

written in 1943 and adopted in 1991, the song previously served as the anthem of the Socialist Republic of Macedonia, when it was part of Yugoslavia
