**Iceland** es un país en Northern Europe, Europe. Su capital es Reykjavik. El himno nacional es *Lofsöngur* ("The Hope").

## Himno Nacional

**Título en inglés:** The Hope

**Compositor:** Sveinbjörn Sveinbjörnsson

**Letrista:** Matthías Jochumsson

adopted 2004, unofficial since 1948; used as the anthem of the Zionist movement since 1897; the 1888 arrangement by Samuel COHEN is thought to be based on the Romanian folk song "Carul cu boi" (The Ox-Driven Cart)
