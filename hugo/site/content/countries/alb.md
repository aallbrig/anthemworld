**Albania** is a country in Southeast Europe, Europe. Its capital is Tirana. The national anthem is *Himni i Flamurit* ("Hymn to the Flag").

## National Anthem

**English title:** Hymn to the Flag

**Composer:** Ciprian Ilieș

**Lyricist:** Aleksander Stavre Drenova

adopted 1912; only the first two stanzas of the original poem are used, with the second stanza as a chorus
