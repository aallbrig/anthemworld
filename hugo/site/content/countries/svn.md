**Slovenia** is a country in Central Europe, Europe. Its capital is Ljubljana. The national anthem is *Zdravljica* ("A Toast").

## National Anthem

**English title:** A Toast

**Composer:** Stanko Premrl

**Lyricist:** France Prešeren

adopted in 1989; originally written in 1848; only the seventh verse of the poem is used as the anthem
