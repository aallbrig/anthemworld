**South Korea** es un país en Eastern Asia, Asia. Su capital es Seoul. El himno nacional es *Aegukga* ("Patriotic Song").

## Himno Nacional

**Título en inglés:** Patriotic Song

**Compositor:** Ahn Eak-tai

**Letrista:** Yun Ch'iho

adopted 1948, well-known by 1910; North Korea's and South Korea's anthems have the same name and a similar melody, but different lyrics
