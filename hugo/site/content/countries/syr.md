**Syria** is a country in Western Asia, Asia. Its capital is Damascus. The national anthem is *Humat ad-Diyar* ("Guardians of the Homeland").

## National Anthem

**English title:** Guardians of the Homeland

**Composer:** Muhammad Fulayfil

**Lyricist:** Khalil Mardam Bey

adopted 1936, restored 1961; the country had a different anthem between 1958 and 1961, when Syria was part of the United Arab Republic
