**Bosnia and Herzegovina** is a country in Southeast Europe, Europe. Its capital is Sarajevo. The national anthem is *national anthem of Bosnia and Herzegovina* ("Our Bahrain").

## National Anthem

**English title:** Our Bahrain

**Composer:** Dušan Šestić

**Lyricist:** Dušan Šestić

adopted 1971; Mohamed Sudqi AYYASH wrote the original lyrics, but they were changed in 2002 after Bahrain became a kingdom
