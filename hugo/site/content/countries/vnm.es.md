**Vietnam** es un país en South-Eastern Asia, Asia. Su capital es Hanoi. El himno nacional es *Tiến Quân Ca* ("The Song of the Marching Troops").

## Himno Nacional

**Título en inglés:** The Song of the Marching Troops

**Compositor:** Văn Cao

**Letrista:** Văn Cao

**Adoptado:** 1944-01-01

adopted as the national anthem of the Democratic Republic of Vietnam in 1945; it became the national anthem of the unified Socialist Republic of Vietnam in 1976; only the first verse is used as the official anthem
