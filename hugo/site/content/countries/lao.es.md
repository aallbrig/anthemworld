**Laos** es un país en South-Eastern Asia, Asia. Su capital es Vientiane. El himno nacional es *Pheng Xat Lao* ("Hymn of the Lao People").

## Himno Nacional

**Título en inglés:** Hymn of the Lao People

**Compositor:** Thongdy Sounthone Vichit

**Letrista:** Sisana Sisane

music adopted 1945, lyrics adopted 1975; the anthem's lyrics were changed after the communist revolution that overthrew the monarchy in 1975
