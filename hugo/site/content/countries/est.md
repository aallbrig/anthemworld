**Estonia** is a country in Northern Europe, Europe. Its capital is Tallinn. The national anthem is *Mu isamaa, mu õnn ja rõõm* ("My Native Land, My Pride and Joy").

## National Anthem

**English title:** My Native Land, My Pride and Joy

**Composer:** Fredrik Pacius

**Lyricist:** Johann Voldemar Jannsen

adopted 1920, but banned between 1940 and 1990 under Soviet occupation; unofficially in use since 1869, it has the same melody as Finland's anthem, but with different lyrics
