**Denmark** es un país en Northern Europe, Europe. Su capital es Copenhagen. El himno nacional es *Kong Christian stod ved højen mast* ("King Christian Stood by the Lofty Mast").

## Himno Nacional

**Título en inglés:** King Christian Stood by the Lofty Mast

**Compositor:** Ditlev Ludvig Rogert

**Letrista:** Johannes Ewald

adopted 1780; one of the oldest royal anthems in the world; used for events when Danish royalty is present; anthem has equal status with the national anthem
