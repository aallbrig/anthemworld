**Turkmenistan** is a country in Central Asia, Asia. Its capital is Ashgabat. The national anthem is *National anthem of Turkmenistan* ("Independent, Neutral, Turkmenistan State Anthem").

## National Anthem

**English title:** Independent, Neutral, Turkmenistan State Anthem

**Composer:** Veli Mukhatov

**Lyricist:** Saparmurat Niyazov

adopted 1997; lyrics revised in 2008 to eliminate references to deceased President Saparmurat NYYAZOW
