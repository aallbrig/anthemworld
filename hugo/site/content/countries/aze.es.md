**Azerbaijan** es un país en Western Asia, Asia. Su capital es Baku. El himno nacional es *Azərbaycan marşı* ("March of Azerbaijan").

## Himno Nacional

**Título en inglés:** March of Azerbaijan

**Compositor:** Uzeyir Hajibeyov

**Letrista:** Ahmad Javad

**Adoptado:** 1918-01-01

adopted 1992; originally written in 1919 during a brief period of independence, but did not become the official anthem until after the dissolution of the Soviet Union
