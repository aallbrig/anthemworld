**Dominican Republic** is a country in Caribbean, Americas. Its capital is Santo Domingo. The national anthem is *National Anthem of the Dominican Republic*.

## National Anthem

**Composer:** José Rufino Reyes y Siancas

**Lyricist:** Emilio Prud’Home

adopted 1934; also known as "Quisqueyanos valientes" (Valiant Sons of Quisqueye); the anthem refers to the Dominican people as Quisqueyanos, which comes from the ethnic name for the island
