**Honduras** is a country in Central America, Americas. Its capital is Tegucigalpa. The national anthem is *National Anthem of Honduras*.

## National Anthem

**Composer:** Carlos Hartling

**Lyricist:** Augusto Coello

adopted 1915; the anthem's seven verses chronicle Honduran history; on official occasions, only the chorus and last verse are sung
