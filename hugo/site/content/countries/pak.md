**Pakistan** is a country in Southern Asia, Asia. Its capital is Islamabad. The national anthem is *Qaumi Taranah*.

## National Anthem

**Composer:** Ahmed Ghulamali Chagla

**Lyricist:** Abu-Al-Asar Hafeez Jullundhri

adopted 1954; also known as "Pak sarzamin shad bad" (Blessed Be the Sacred Land)
