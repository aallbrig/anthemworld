**Kiribati** is a country in Micronesia, Oceania. Its capital is South Tarawa. The national anthem is *Kunan Kiribati*.

## National Anthem

**Composer:** Ioteba Tamuera Uriam

**Lyricist:** Ioteba Tamuera Uriam

**Adopted:** 1979-01-01
