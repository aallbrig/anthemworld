**突尼斯** 是一个国家，位于 Northern Africa, Africa，首都是 Tunis，国歌是 *Humat Al Hima* ("Song of the King of the Tonga Islands")。

## 国歌

**英文名称：** Song of the King of the Tonga Islands

**作曲：** Mohammed Abdel Wahab

**作词：** Mostafa Saadeq Al-Rafe'ie

in use since 1874; more commonly known as "Fasi Fakafonua" (National Song)
