**North Macedonia** es un país en Southeast Europe, Europe. Su capital es Skopje. El himno nacional es *Denes nad Makedonija* ("Today Over Macedonia").

## Himno Nacional

**Título en inglés:** Today Over Macedonia

**Compositor:** Todor Skalovski

**Letrista:** Vlado Maleski

written in 1943 and adopted in 1991, the song previously served as the anthem of the Socialist Republic of Macedonia, when it was part of Yugoslavia
