**苏里南** 是一个国家，位于 South America, Americas，首都是 Paramaribo，国歌是 *God zij met ons Suriname* ("God Be With Our Suriname")。

## 国歌

**英文名称：** God Be With Our Suriname

**作曲：** Johannes Corstianus de Puy

**作词：** Cornelis Atzes Hoekstra

adopted 1959; originally adapted from a Sunday-school song written in 1893; contains lyrics in both Dutch and Sranang Tongo
