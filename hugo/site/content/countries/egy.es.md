**Egypt** es un país en Northern Africa, Africa. Su capital es Cairo. El himno nacional es *Bilady, Bilady, Bilady* ("My Homeland, My Homeland, My Homeland").

## Himno Nacional

**Título en inglés:** My Homeland, My Homeland, My Homeland

**Compositor:** Sayyid Darwish

**Letrista:** Yunes Elqadi

adopted 1979; the current anthem was written after the 1979 peace treaty with Israel; the composer is considered the father of modern Egyptian music; of the three verses, only the first verse is sung, preceded and followed by the chorus
