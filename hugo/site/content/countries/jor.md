**Jordan** is a country in Western Asia, Asia. Its capital is Amman. The national anthem is *The Royal Anthem of Jordan* ("Long Live the King of Jordan").

## National Anthem

**English title:** Long Live the King of Jordan

**Composer:** ʿAbdul Qādir Tanīr

**Lyricist:** Abdelmunim al-Rifai

adopted 1946; the shortened version of the anthem is most commonly used; the full version is reserved for special occasions
