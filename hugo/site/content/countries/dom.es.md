**Dominican Republic** es un país en Caribbean, Americas. Su capital es Santo Domingo. El himno nacional es *National Anthem of the Dominican Republic*.

## Himno Nacional

**Compositor:** José Rufino Reyes y Siancas

**Letrista:** Emilio Prud’Home

adopted 1934; also known as "Quisqueyanos valientes" (Valiant Sons of Quisqueye); the anthem refers to the Dominican people as Quisqueyanos, which comes from the ethnic name for the island
