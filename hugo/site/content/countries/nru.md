**Nauru** is a country in Micronesia, Oceania. Its capital is Yaren. The national anthem is *Nauru Bwiema* ("Nauru, Our Homeland").

## National Anthem

**English title:** Nauru, Our Homeland

**Composer:** Laurence Henry Hicks

**Lyricist:** Margaret Hendrie

**Adopted:** 1968
