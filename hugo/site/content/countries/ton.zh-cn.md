**汤加** 是一个国家，位于 Polynesia, Oceania，首都是 Nuku'alofa，国歌是 *Ko e fasi 'o e tu'i 'o e 'Otu Tonga* ("Hail to Thee, Land of Our Forefathers")。

## 国歌

**英文名称：** Hail to Thee, Land of Our Forefathers

**作曲：** Carl Schmitt

adopted 1960, restored 1992; anthem was replaced during one-party rule between 1979 and 1992
