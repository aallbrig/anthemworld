**Hungary** is a country in Central Europe, Europe. Its capital is Budapest. The national anthem is *Himnusz* ("Hymn").

## National Anthem

**English title:** Hymn

**Composer:** Ferenc Erkel

**Lyricist:** Ferenc Kölcsey
