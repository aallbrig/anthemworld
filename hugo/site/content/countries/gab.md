**Gabon** is a country in Middle Africa, Africa. Its capital is Libreville. The national anthem is *La Concorde*.

## National Anthem

**Composer:** Georges Aleka Damas

**Lyricist:** Georges Aleka Damas

adopted 1965; the music is an adaptation of the traditional Mandinka song "Foday Kaba Dumbuya"
