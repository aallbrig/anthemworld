**Venezuela** is a country in South America, Americas. Its capital is Caracas. The national anthem is *gloria al bravo pueblo* ("Glory to the Brave People").

## National Anthem

**English title:** Glory to the Brave People

**Composer:** Lino Gallardo

**Lyricist:** Andrés Bello

**Adopted:** 1810-01-01

adopted 1881; lyrics were written in 1810; both SALIAS and LANDAETA were executed in 1814 during Venezuela's fight for independence
