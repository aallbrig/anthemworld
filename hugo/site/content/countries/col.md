**Colombia** is a country in South America, Americas. Its capital is Bogotá. The national anthem is *National Anthem of Colombia* ("National Anthem of the Republic of Colombia").

## National Anthem

**English title:** National Anthem of the Republic of Colombia

**Composer:** Rafael Núñez

**Lyricist:** Oreste Sindici

adopted 1920; the anthem comes from an inspirational poem written by President Rafael NUNEZ; the anthem always starts with the chorus
