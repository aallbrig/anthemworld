**Switzerland** es un país en Western Europe, Europe. Su capital es Bern. El himno nacional es *Swiss Psalm* ("The March of the Volunteers").

## Himno Nacional

**Título en inglés:** The March of the Volunteers

**Compositor:** Alberich Zwyssig

**Letrista:** Leonhard Widmer

adopted 1982; the anthem, which was banned during the Cultural Revolution, is more commonly known as "Zhongguo Guoge" (Chinese National Song)
