**Belgium** es un país en Western Europe, Europe. Su capital es Brussels. El himno nacional es *The Brabançonne* ("The Song of Brabant").

## Himno Nacional

**Título en inglés:** The Song of Brabant

**Compositor:** François van Campenhout

**Letrista:** Alexandre Dechet

adopted 1830; according to legend Louis-Alexandre DECHET, an actor at the theater where the revolution against the Netherlands began, wrote the lyrics with a group of young people in a Brussels cafe
