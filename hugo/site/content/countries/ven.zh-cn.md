**委内瑞拉** 是一个国家，位于 South America, Americas，首都是 Caracas，国歌是 *gloria al bravo pueblo* ("Glory to the Brave People")。

## 国歌

**英文名称：** Glory to the Brave People

**作曲：** Lino Gallardo

**作词：** Andrés Bello

**采用时间：** 1810-01-01

adopted 1881; lyrics were written in 1810; both SALIAS and LANDAETA were executed in 1814 during Venezuela's fight for independence
