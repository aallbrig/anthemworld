**Slovakia** is a country in Central Europe, Europe. Its capital is Bratislava. The national anthem is *Nad Tatrou sa blýska* ("Storm Over the Tatras").

## National Anthem

**English title:** Storm Over the Tatras

**Lyricist:** Janko Matúška

adopted 1993; music based on an 1843 Slovak folk song "Kopala studienku" (She Was Digging a Well)
