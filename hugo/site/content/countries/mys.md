**Malaysia** is a country in South-Eastern Asia, Asia. Its capital is Kuala Lumpur. The national anthem is *Negaraku* ("My Country").

## National Anthem

**English title:** My Country

**Composer:** Pierre-Jean de Béranger

**Lyricist:** Saiful Bahri

adopted 1957; full version only performed in the king's presence, the shorter version performed for the queen and lesser officials
