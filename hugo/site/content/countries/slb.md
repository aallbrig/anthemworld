**Solomon Islands** is a country in Melanesia, Oceania. Its capital is Honiara. The national anthem is *God Save Our Solomon Islands* ("The Song of Marseille").

## National Anthem

**English title:** The Song of Marseille

**Composer:** Panapasa Balekana

**Lyricist:** Panapasa Balekana
