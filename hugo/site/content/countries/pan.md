**Panama** is a country in Central America, Americas. Its capital is Panama City. The national anthem is *National anthem of Panama* ("Paraguayans, the Republic or Death!").

## National Anthem

**English title:** Paraguayans, the Republic or Death!

**Composer:** Santos Jorge

**Lyricist:** Jeronimo de la Ossa

adopted 1846 (lyrics) and 1934 (music)
