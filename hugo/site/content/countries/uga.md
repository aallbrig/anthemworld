**Uganda** is a country in Eastern Africa, Africa. Its capital is Kampala. The national anthem is *Oh Uganda, Land of Beauty*.

## National Anthem

**Composer:** George Wilberforce Kakoma

**Lyricist:** George Wilberforce Kakoma

adopted 1962; one of the shortest national anthems in the world
