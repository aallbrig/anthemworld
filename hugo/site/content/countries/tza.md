**Tanzania** is a country in Eastern Africa, Africa. Its capital is Dodoma. The national anthem is *Mungu ibariki Afrika* ("God Bless Africa").

**English title:** God Bless Africa

**Composer:** Enoch Mankayi Sontonga

**Lyricist:** collective
