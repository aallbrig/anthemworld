**Denmark** is a country in Northern Europe, Europe. Its capital is Copenhagen. The national anthem is *Kong Christian stod ved højen mast* ("King Christian Stood by the Lofty Mast").

## National Anthem

**English title:** King Christian Stood by the Lofty Mast

**Composer:** Ditlev Ludvig Rogert

**Lyricist:** Johannes Ewald

adopted 1780; one of the oldest royal anthems in the world; used for events when Danish royalty is present; anthem has equal status with the national anthem
