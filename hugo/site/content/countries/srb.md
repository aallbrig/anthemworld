**Serbia** is a country in Southeast Europe, Europe. Its capital is Belgrade. The national anthem is *Bože pravde* ("God of Justice").

## National Anthem

**English title:** God of Justice

**Composer:** Davorin Jenko

**Lyricist:** Jovan Đorđević

adopted 1904; song originally written as part of a play in 1872, and the Serbian people have used it as an anthem in the 20th and 21st centuries
