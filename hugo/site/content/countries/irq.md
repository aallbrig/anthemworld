**Iraq** is a country in Western Asia, Asia. Its capital is Baghdad. The national anthem is *Mawtini* ("My Homeland").

## National Anthem

**English title:** My Homeland

**Lyricist:** Ibrāhīm tooqān

adopted 2004, after the ouster of SADDAM Husayn; popular Arab folk song that also serves as an unofficial anthem for the Palestinian people
