**Singapore** is a country in South-Eastern Asia, Asia. Its capital is Singapore. The national anthem is *Onward Singapore* ("Pluck Your Koras, Strike the Balafons").

## National Anthem

**English title:** Pluck Your Koras, Strike the Balafons

**Composer:** Zubir Said

**Lyricist:** Zubir Said

adopted 1960; lyrics written by Leopold Sedar SENGHOR, Senegal's first president; the anthem sometimes played incorporating the koras (harp-like stringed instruments) and balafons (types of xylophones) mentioned in the title
