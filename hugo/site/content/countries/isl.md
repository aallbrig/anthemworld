**Iceland** is a country in Northern Europe, Europe. Its capital is Reykjavik. The national anthem is *Lofsöngur* ("The Hope").

## National Anthem

**English title:** The Hope

**Composer:** Sveinbjörn Sveinbjörnsson

**Lyricist:** Matthías Jochumsson

adopted 2004, unofficial since 1948; used as the anthem of the Zionist movement since 1897; the 1888 arrangement by Samuel COHEN is thought to be based on the Romanian folk song "Carul cu boi" (The Ox-Driven Cart)
