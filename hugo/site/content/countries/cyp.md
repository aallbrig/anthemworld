**Cyprus** is a country in Southern Europe, Europe. Its capital is Nicosia. The national anthem is *Hymn to Liberty* ("Hymn to Freedom").

## National Anthem

**English title:** Hymn to Freedom

**Composer:** Nikolaos Mantzaros

**Lyricist:** Dionysios Solomos

**Adopted:** 1823-01-01

adopted 1966; Cyprus uses the Greek national anthem; the Turkish Cypriot community in Cyprus uses Turkey's national anthem
