**South Korea** is a country in Eastern Asia, Asia. Its capital is Seoul. The national anthem is *Aegukga* ("Patriotic Song").

## National Anthem

**English title:** Patriotic Song

**Composer:** Ahn Eak-tai

**Lyricist:** Yun Ch'iho

adopted 1948, well-known by 1910; North Korea's and South Korea's anthems have the same name and a similar melody, but different lyrics
