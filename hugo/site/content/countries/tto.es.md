**Trinidad and Tobago** es un país en Caribbean, Americas. Su capital es Port of Spain. El himno nacional es *Forged from the Love of Liberty* ("Fatherland").

## Himno Nacional

**Título en inglés:** Fatherland

**Compositor:** Patrick Castagne

**Letrista:** Patrick Castagne

adopted 2002; the song was first used as an anthem when Timor-Leste declared its independence from Portugal in 1975; the lyricist, Francisco Borja DA COSTA, was killed in the Indonesian invasion just days after independence was declared
