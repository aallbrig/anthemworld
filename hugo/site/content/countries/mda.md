**Moldova** is a country in Eastern Europe, Europe. Its capital is Chișinău. The national anthem is *Limba noastră* ("Our Tongue").

## National Anthem

**English title:** Our Tongue

**Composer:** Alexandru Cristea

**Lyricist:** Alexei Mateevici

adopted 1994; originally a 12-verse poem, but only stanzas 1, 2, 5, 9, and 12 are included in the anthem
