**Tajikistan** is a country in Central Asia, Asia. Its capital is Dushanbe. The national anthem is *National anthem of Tajikistan*.

## National Anthem

**Composer:** Suleiman Yudakov

**Lyricist:** Gulnazar Keldi

adopted 1994; after the fall of the Soviet Union, Tajikistan kept the music of its Soviet-era anthem, but adopted new lyrics
