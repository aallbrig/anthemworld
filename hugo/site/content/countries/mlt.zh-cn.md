**马耳他** 是一个国家，位于 Southern Europe, Europe，首都是 Valletta，国歌是 *L-Innu Malti* ("The Hymn of Malta")。

## 国歌

**英文名称：** The Hymn of Malta

**作曲：** Robert Samut

**作词：** Dun Karm Psaila

adopted 1945; written in the form of a prayer to bind together the political parties and the country
