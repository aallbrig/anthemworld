**摩尔多瓦** 是一个国家，位于 Eastern Europe, Europe，首都是 Chișinău，国歌是 *Limba noastră* ("Our Tongue")。

## 国歌

**英文名称：** Our Tongue

**作曲：** Alexandru Cristea

**作词：** Alexei Mateevici

adopted 1994; originally a 12-verse poem, but only stanzas 1, 2, 5, 9, and 12 are included in the anthem
