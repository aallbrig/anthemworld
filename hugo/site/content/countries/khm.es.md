**Cambodia** es un país en South-Eastern Asia, Asia. Su capital es Phnom Penh. El himno nacional es *Nokor Reach* ("Royal Kingdom").

## Himno Nacional

**Título en inglés:** Royal Kingdom

**Compositor:** Norodom Suramarit

**Letrista:** Chuon Nath

adopted 1941, restored 1993; the anthem, based on a Cambodian folk tune, was restored after the defeat of the Communist regime
