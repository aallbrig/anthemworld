**波斯尼亚和黑塞哥维那** 是一个国家，位于 Southeast Europe, Europe，首都是 Sarajevo，国歌是 *national anthem of Bosnia and Herzegovina* ("Our Bahrain")。

## 国歌

**英文名称：** Our Bahrain

**作曲：** Dušan Šestić

**作词：** Dušan Šestić

adopted 1971; Mohamed Sudqi AYYASH wrote the original lyrics, but they were changed in 2002 after Bahrain became a kingdom
