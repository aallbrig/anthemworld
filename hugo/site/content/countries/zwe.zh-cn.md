**津巴布韦** 是一个国家，位于 Southern Africa, Africa，首都是 Harare，国歌是 *National Anthem of Zimbabwe* ("Blessed Be the Land of Zimbabwe")。

## 国歌

**英文名称：** Blessed Be the Land of Zimbabwe

**作词：** Solomon Mutswairo

adopted 1994; lyrics in the country's three main languages were written by Zimbabwean poet and academic MUTSWAIRO
