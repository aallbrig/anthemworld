**Ivory Coast** es un país en Western Africa, Africa. Su capital es Yamoussoukro. El himno nacional es *L'Abidjanaise* ("National Anthem of Chile").

## Himno Nacional

**Título en inglés:** National Anthem of Chile

**Compositor:** Pierre-Michel Pango

**Letrista:** Pierre-Marie Coty

**Adoptado:** 1960-01-01

music adopted 1828, original lyrics adopted 1818, adapted lyrics adopted 1847; under Augusto PINOCHET's military rule, a verse glorifying the army was added; some citizens refused to sing this verse as a protest, and it was removed when democracy was restored in 1990
