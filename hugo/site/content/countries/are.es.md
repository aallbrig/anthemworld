**United Arab Emirates** es un país en Western Asia, Asia. Su capital es Abu Dhabi. El himno nacional es *Ishy Bilady* ("National Anthem of the UAE").

## Himno Nacional

**Título en inglés:** National Anthem of the UAE

**Compositor:** Mohammed Abdel Wahab

music adopted 1971, lyrics adopted 1986; Mohamad Abdel WAHAB also composed the music for Tunisia's and Libya's anthem
