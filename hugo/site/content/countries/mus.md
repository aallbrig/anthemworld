**Mauritius** is a country in Eastern Africa, Africa. Its capital is Port Louis. The national anthem is *Motherland* ("The Sultan's Anthem").

## National Anthem

**English title:** The Sultan's Anthem

**Composer:** Joseph Philippe Gentil

**Lyricist:** Jean-Georges Prosper

adopted 1932; new lyrics written after QABOOS bin Said al Said came to power in 1970; first performed by the band of the HMS Hawkins as a salute to the Sultan during a 1932 visit to Muscat; the ship's bandmaster did the arrangement
