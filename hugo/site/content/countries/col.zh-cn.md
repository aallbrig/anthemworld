**哥伦比亚** 是一个国家，位于 South America, Americas，首都是 Bogotá，国歌是 *National Anthem of Colombia* ("National Anthem of the Republic of Colombia")。

## 国歌

**英文名称：** National Anthem of the Republic of Colombia

**作曲：** Rafael Núñez

**作词：** Oreste Sindici

adopted 1920; the anthem comes from an inspirational poem written by President Rafael NUNEZ; the anthem always starts with the chorus
