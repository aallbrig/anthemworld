**Saint Vincent and the Grenadines** is a country in Caribbean, Americas. Its capital is Kingstown. The national anthem is *Saint Vincent, Land so Beautiful*.

**Composer:** Joel Bertram Miguel

**Lyricist:** Phyllis Joyce McClean Punnett
