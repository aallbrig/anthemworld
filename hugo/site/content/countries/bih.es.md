**Bosnia and Herzegovina** es un país en Southeast Europe, Europe. Su capital es Sarajevo. El himno nacional es *national anthem of Bosnia and Herzegovina* ("Our Bahrain").

## Himno Nacional

**Título en inglés:** Our Bahrain

**Compositor:** Dušan Šestić

**Letrista:** Dušan Šestić

adopted 1971; Mohamed Sudqi AYYASH wrote the original lyrics, but they were changed in 2002 after Bahrain became a kingdom
