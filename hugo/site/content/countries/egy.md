**Egypt** is a country in Northern Africa, Africa. Its capital is Cairo. The national anthem is *Bilady, Bilady, Bilady* ("My Homeland, My Homeland, My Homeland").

## National Anthem

**English title:** My Homeland, My Homeland, My Homeland

**Composer:** Sayyid Darwish

**Lyricist:** Yunes Elqadi

adopted 1979; the current anthem was written after the 1979 peace treaty with Israel; the composer is considered the father of modern Egyptian music; of the three verses, only the first verse is sung, preceded and followed by the chorus
