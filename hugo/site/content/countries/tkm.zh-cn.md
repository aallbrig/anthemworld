**土库曼斯坦** 是一个国家，位于 Central Asia, Asia，首都是 Ashgabat，国歌是 *National anthem of Turkmenistan* ("Independent, Neutral, Turkmenistan State Anthem")。

## 国歌

**英文名称：** Independent, Neutral, Turkmenistan State Anthem

**作曲：** Veli Mukhatov

**作词：** Saparmurat Niyazov

adopted 1997; lyrics revised in 2008 to eliminate references to deceased President Saparmurat NYYAZOW
