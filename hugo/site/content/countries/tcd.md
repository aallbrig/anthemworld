**Chad** is a country in Middle Africa, Africa. Its capital is N'Djamena. The national anthem is *La Tchadienne*.

## National Anthem

**Composer:** Paul Vilalrd

**Lyricist:** Louis Gidrol

adopted 1962; song originally written as an anthem for the West Indies Federation; Trinidad and Tobago adopted it when the Federation dissolved
