**Saint Kitts and Nevis** is a country in Caribbean, Americas. Its capital is Basseterre. The national anthem is *O Land of Beauty!* ("Patriotic Song").

## National Anthem

**English title:** Patriotic Song

**Composer:** Kenrick Georges

**Lyricist:** Kenrick Georges
