**Zimbabwe** is a country in Southern Africa, Africa. Its capital is Harare. The national anthem is *National Anthem of Zimbabwe* ("Blessed Be the Land of Zimbabwe").

## National Anthem

**English title:** Blessed Be the Land of Zimbabwe

**Lyricist:** Solomon Mutswairo

adopted 1994; lyrics in the country's three main languages were written by Zimbabwean poet and academic MUTSWAIRO
