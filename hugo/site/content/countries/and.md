**Andorra** is a country in Southern Europe, Europe. Its capital is Andorra la Vella. The national anthem is *El gran Carlemany* ("The Great Charlemagne").

## National Anthem

**English title:** The Great Charlemagne

**Composer:** Enric Marfany Bons

**Lyricist:** Juan Benlloch i Vivó

adopted 1921; the anthem provides a brief history of Andorra in a first-person narrative
