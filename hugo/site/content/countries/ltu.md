**Lithuania** is a country in Northern Europe, Europe. Its capital is Vilnius. The national anthem is *Tautiška giesmė* ("The National Song").

## National Anthem

**English title:** The National Song

**Composer:** Vincas Kudirka

**Lyricist:** Vincas Kudirka

adopted 1918, restored 1990; written in 1898 when Lithuania was part of Russia; banned during the Soviet occupation from 1940 to 1990
