**Ukraine** es un país en Eastern Europe, Europe. Su capital es Kyiv. El himno nacional es *Shche ne vmerla Ukrainy i slava, i volia* ("Ukraine Has Not Yet Perished").

## Himno Nacional

**Título en inglés:** Ukraine Has Not Yet Perished

**Compositor:** Mykhailo Verbytskyi

**Letrista:** Pavlo Chubynskyi

**Adoptado:** 2003-03-06

music adopted 1991, lyrics adopted 2003; current version of the anthem is the first verse of CHUBYNSKYI's poem, plus the chorus
