**Eswatini** is a country in Southern Africa, Africa. Its capital is Mbabane. The national anthem is *Nkulunkulu Mnikati wetibusiso temaSwati* ("Swiss Psalm").

## National Anthem

**English title:** Swiss Psalm

adopted 1981; all four of the versions (German, French, Italian, Romansch) are considered official
