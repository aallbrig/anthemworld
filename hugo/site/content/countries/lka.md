**Sri Lanka** is a country in Southern Asia, Asia. Its capital is Sri Jayawardenepura Kotte. The national anthem is *Sri Lanka Matha* ("Mother Sri Lanka").

## National Anthem

**English title:** Mother Sri Lanka

**Composer:** Ananda Samarakoon

**Lyricist:** Ananda Samarakoon

**Adopted:** 1951
