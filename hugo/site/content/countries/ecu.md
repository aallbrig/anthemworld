**Ecuador** is a country in South America, Americas. Its capital is Quito. The national anthem is *Salve, Oh Patria* ("We Salute You, Our Homeland").

## National Anthem

**English title:** We Salute You, Our Homeland

**Composer:** Antonio Neumane

**Lyricist:** Juan León Mera

adopted 1948; MERA wrote the lyrics in 1865; only the chorus and second verse are sung
