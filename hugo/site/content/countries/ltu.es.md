**Lithuania** es un país en Northern Europe, Europe. Su capital es Vilnius. El himno nacional es *Tautiška giesmė* ("The National Song").

## Himno Nacional

**Título en inglés:** The National Song

**Compositor:** Vincas Kudirka

**Letrista:** Vincas Kudirka

adopted 1918, restored 1990; written in 1898 when Lithuania was part of Russia; banned during the Soviet occupation from 1940 to 1990
