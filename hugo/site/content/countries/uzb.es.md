**Uzbekistan** es un país en Central Asia, Asia. Su capital es Tashkent. El himno nacional es *National Anthem of Uzbekistan* ("National Anthem of the Republic of Uzbekistan").

## Himno Nacional

**Título en inglés:** National Anthem of the Republic of Uzbekistan

**Compositor:** Mutal Burhonov

**Letrista:** Abdulla Oripov

adopted 1992; after the fall of the Soviet Union, Uzbekistan kept the music of its Soviet-era anthem but adopted new lyrics
