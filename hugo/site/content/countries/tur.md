**Turkey** is a country in Western Asia, Asia. Its capital is Ankara. The national anthem is *İstiklâl Marşı* ("The March of Independence").

## National Anthem

**English title:** The March of Independence

**Composer:** Osman Zeki Üngör

**Lyricist:** Mehmet Âkif Ersoy

lyrics adopted 1921, music adopted 1932; the anthem's original music was adopted in 1924
