**Nepal** is a country in Southern Asia, Asia. Its capital is Kathmandu. The national anthem is *Sayaun Thunga Phulka* ("Hundreds of Flowers").

## National Anthem

**English title:** Hundreds of Flowers

**Composer:** Amber Gurung

**Lyricist:** Byakul Maila

**Adopted:** 2007
