**France** is a country in Western Europe, Europe. Its capital is Paris. The national anthem is *La Marseillaise* ("The Song of Marseille").

## National Anthem

**English title:** The Song of Marseille

**Composer:** Claude Joseph Rouget de Lisle

**Lyricist:** Claude Joseph Rouget de Lisle

**Adopted:** 1792-05-06

adopted 1795, restored 1870; acquired its name when the National Guard of Marseille sang the song while marching into Paris in 1792 during the French Revolution; one of the most recognized anthems in the world
