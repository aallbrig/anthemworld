**Costa Rica** is a country in Central America, Americas. Its capital is San José. The national anthem is *Noble patria, tu hermosa bandera* ("National Anthem of Costa Rica").

## National Anthem

**English title:** National Anthem of Costa Rica

**Composer:** Manuel María Gutiérrez

**Lyricist:** José María Zeledón Brenes

adopted 1949; the music was originally written for a welcome ceremony in 1852 for the US and UK diplomatic missions; the lyrics were added in 1900
