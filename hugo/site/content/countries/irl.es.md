**Ireland** es un país en Northern Europe, Europe. Su capital es Dublin. El himno nacional es *Amhrán na bhFiann* ("The Soldier's Song").

## Himno Nacional

**Título en inglés:** The Soldier's Song

**Compositor:** Patrick Heeney

**Letrista:** Peadar Kearney

adopted 1926; the song "Ireland's Call" is often used as the anthem at athletic events if citizens of Ireland and Northern Ireland are competing as a unified team
