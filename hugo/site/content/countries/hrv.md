**Croatia** is a country in Southeast Europe, Europe. Its capital is Zagreb. The national anthem is *Lijepa naša domovino* ("Our Beautiful Homeland").

## National Anthem

**English title:** Our Beautiful Homeland

**Composer:** Josip Runjanin

**Lyricist:** Antun Mihanović

adopted in 1972 while still part of Yugoslavia; the lyrics were written in 1835, and it served as an unofficial anthem beginning in 1891
