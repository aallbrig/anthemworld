**Sweden** is a country in Northern Europe, Europe. Its capital is Stockholm. The national anthem is *Du gamla, du fria* ("Royal Song").

## National Anthem

**English title:** Royal Song

**Lyricist:** Richard Dybeck

adopted 1844 as the royal anthem, but also used as the national anthem until 1893; only the first verse is sung if the monarch is present
