**Costa Rica** es un país en Central America, Americas. Su capital es San José. El himno nacional es *Noble patria, tu hermosa bandera* ("National Anthem of Costa Rica").

## Himno Nacional

**Título en inglés:** National Anthem of Costa Rica

**Compositor:** Manuel María Gutiérrez

**Letrista:** José María Zeledón Brenes

adopted 1949; the music was originally written for a welcome ceremony in 1852 for the US and UK diplomatic missions; the lyrics were added in 1900
