**Uzbekistan** is a country in Central Asia, Asia. Its capital is Tashkent. The national anthem is *National Anthem of Uzbekistan* ("National Anthem of the Republic of Uzbekistan").

## National Anthem

**English title:** National Anthem of the Republic of Uzbekistan

**Composer:** Mutal Burhonov

**Lyricist:** Abdulla Oripov

adopted 1992; after the fall of the Soviet Union, Uzbekistan kept the music of its Soviet-era anthem but adopted new lyrics
