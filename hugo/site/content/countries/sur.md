**Suriname** is a country in South America, Americas. Its capital is Paramaribo. The national anthem is *God zij met ons Suriname* ("God Be With Our Suriname").

## National Anthem

**English title:** God Be With Our Suriname

**Composer:** Johannes Corstianus de Puy

**Lyricist:** Cornelis Atzes Hoekstra

adopted 1959; originally adapted from a Sunday-school song written in 1893; contains lyrics in both Dutch and Sranang Tongo
