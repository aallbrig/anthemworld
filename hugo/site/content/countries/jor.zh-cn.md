**约旦** 是一个国家，位于 Western Asia, Asia，首都是 Amman，国歌是 *The Royal Anthem of Jordan* ("Long Live the King of Jordan")。

## 国歌

**英文名称：** Long Live the King of Jordan

**作曲：** ʿAbdul Qādir Tanīr

**作词：** Abdelmunim al-Rifai

adopted 1946; the shortened version of the anthem is most commonly used; the full version is reserved for special occasions
