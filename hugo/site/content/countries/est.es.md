**Estonia** es un país en Northern Europe, Europe. Su capital es Tallinn. El himno nacional es *Mu isamaa, mu õnn ja rõõm* ("My Native Land, My Pride and Joy").

## Himno Nacional

**Título en inglés:** My Native Land, My Pride and Joy

**Compositor:** Fredrik Pacius

**Letrista:** Johann Voldemar Jannsen

adopted 1920, but banned between 1940 and 1990 under Soviet occupation; unofficially in use since 1869, it has the same melody as Finland's anthem, but with different lyrics
