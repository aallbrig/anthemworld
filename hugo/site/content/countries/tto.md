**Trinidad and Tobago** is a country in Caribbean, Americas. Its capital is Port of Spain. The national anthem is *Forged from the Love of Liberty* ("Fatherland").

## National Anthem

**English title:** Fatherland

**Composer:** Patrick Castagne

**Lyricist:** Patrick Castagne

adopted 2002; the song was first used as an anthem when Timor-Leste declared its independence from Portugal in 1975; the lyricist, Francisco Borja DA COSTA, was killed in the Indonesian invasion just days after independence was declared
