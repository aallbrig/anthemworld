**苏丹** 是一个国家，位于 Northern Africa, Africa，首都是 Khartoum，国歌是 *Nahnu Jund Allah Jund Al-watan* ("We Are the Army of God and of Our Land")。

## 国歌

**英文名称：** We Are the Army of God and of Our Land

**作词：** Ahmed Mohamed Saleh

adopted 1956; originally served as the anthem of the Sudanese military
