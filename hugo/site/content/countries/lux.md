**Luxembourg** is a country in Western Europe, Europe. Its capital is Luxembourg. The national anthem is *Ons Heemecht* ("The William").

## National Anthem

**English title:** The William

**Composer:** Jean Antoine Zinnen

**Lyricist:** Michel Lentz

adopted 1919; royal anthem, for use when members of the grand ducal family enter or exit a ceremony in Luxembourg
