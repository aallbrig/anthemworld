**Botswana** is a country in Southern Africa, Africa. Its capital is Gaborone. The national anthem is *Fatshe leno la rona* ("Our Land").

## National Anthem

**English title:** Our Land

**Composer:** Kgalemang Tumediso Motsete

**Adopted:** 1966
