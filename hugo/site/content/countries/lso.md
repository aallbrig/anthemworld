**Lesotho** is a country in Southern Africa, Africa. Its capital is Maseru. The national anthem is *Lesotho Fatse La Bontata Rona* ("High Above the Young Rhine").

## National Anthem

**English title:** High Above the Young Rhine

**Composer:** Ferdinand Samuel Laur

**Lyricist:** Joshua Pulomo Mohapeloa

adopted 1850, revised 1963; uses the tune of the United Kingdom's anthem, "God Save the King"
