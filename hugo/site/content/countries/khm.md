**Cambodia** is a country in South-Eastern Asia, Asia. Its capital is Phnom Penh. The national anthem is *Nokor Reach* ("Royal Kingdom").

## National Anthem

**English title:** Royal Kingdom

**Composer:** Norodom Suramarit

**Lyricist:** Chuon Nath

adopted 1941, restored 1993; the anthem, based on a Cambodian folk tune, was restored after the defeat of the Communist regime
