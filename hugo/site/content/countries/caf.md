**Central African Republic** is a country in Middle Africa, Africa. Its capital is Bangui. The national anthem is *La Renaissance* ("The Renaissance").

## National Anthem

**English title:** The Renaissance

**Composer:** Herbert Pepper

**Lyricist:** Barthélemy Boganda

adopted 1960; BOGANDA wrote the anthem's lyrics and was the first prime minister of the autonomous French territory
