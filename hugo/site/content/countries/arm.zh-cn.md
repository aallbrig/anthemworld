**亚美尼亚** 是一个国家，位于 Western Asia, Asia，首都是 Yerevan，国歌是 *Mer Hayrenik* ("Our Fatherland")。

## 国歌

**英文名称：** Our Fatherland

**作曲：** Barsegh Kanachyan

**作词：** Mikael Nalbandian

adopted 1991; based on the anthem of the Democratic Republic of Armenia (1918-1922), but with different lyrics
