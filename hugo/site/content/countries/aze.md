**Azerbaijan** is a country in Western Asia, Asia. Its capital is Baku. The national anthem is *Azərbaycan marşı* ("March of Azerbaijan").

## National Anthem

**English title:** March of Azerbaijan

**Composer:** Uzeyir Hajibeyov

**Lyricist:** Ahmad Javad

**Adopted:** 1918-01-01

adopted 1992; originally written in 1919 during a brief period of independence, but did not become the official anthem until after the dissolution of the Soviet Union
