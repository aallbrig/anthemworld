**Sweden** es un país en Northern Europe, Europe. Su capital es Stockholm. El himno nacional es *Du gamla, du fria* ("Royal Song").

## Himno Nacional

**Título en inglés:** Royal Song

**Letrista:** Richard Dybeck

adopted 1844 as the royal anthem, but also used as the national anthem until 1893; only the first verse is sung if the monarch is present
