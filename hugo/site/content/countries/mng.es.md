**Mongolia** es un país en Eastern Asia, Asia. Su capital es Ulan Bator. El himno nacional es *National anthem of Mongolia* ("The March of Monaco").

## Himno Nacional

**Título en inglés:** The March of Monaco

**Compositor:** Luvsanjambyn Mördorj

**Letrista:** Tsendiin Damdinsüren

**Adoptado:** 1950-01-01

music adopted 1867, lyrics adopted 1931; only the Monegasque lyrics are official; the French version is known as "Hymne Monegasque" (Monegasque Anthem); the words are usually only sung on official occasions
