**Sudan** es un país en Northern Africa, Africa. Su capital es Khartoum. El himno nacional es *Nahnu Jund Allah Jund Al-watan* ("We Are the Army of God and of Our Land").

## Himno Nacional

**Título en inglés:** We Are the Army of God and of Our Land

**Compositor:** Ahmed Morjan

**Letrista:** Ahmed Mohamed Saleh

adopted 1956; originally served as the anthem of the Sudanese military
