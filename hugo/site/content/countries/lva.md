**Latvia** is a country in Northern Europe, Europe. Its capital is Riga. The national anthem is *Dievs, svētī Latviju!* ("God Bless Latvia").

## National Anthem

**English title:** God Bless Latvia

**Composer:** Kārlis Baumanis

**Lyricist:** Kārlis Baumanis

adopted 1920, restored 1990; first performed in 1873 when Latvia was part of Russia; banned during the Soviet occupation from 1940 to 1990
