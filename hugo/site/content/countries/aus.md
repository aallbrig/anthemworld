**Australia** is a country in Australia and New Zealand, Oceania. Its capital is Canberra. The national anthem is *Advance Australia Fair* ("Federal Hymn").

## National Anthem

**English title:** Federal Hymn

**Composer:** Peter Dodds McCormick

**Lyricist:** Peter Dodds McCormick

**Adopted:** 1878-11-01
