**Mauritius** es un país en Eastern Africa, Africa. Su capital es Port Louis. El himno nacional es *Motherland* ("The Sultan's Anthem").

## Himno Nacional

**Título en inglés:** The Sultan's Anthem

**Compositor:** Joseph Philippe Gentil

**Letrista:** Jean-Georges Prosper

adopted 1932; new lyrics written after QABOOS bin Said al Said came to power in 1970; first performed by the band of the HMS Hawkins as a salute to the Sultan during a 1932 visit to Muscat; the ship's bandmaster did the arrangement
