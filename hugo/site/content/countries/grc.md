**Greece** is a country in Southern Europe, Europe. Its capital is Athens. The national anthem is *Hymn to Liberty* ("Hymn to Freedom").

## National Anthem

**English title:** Hymn to Freedom

**Composer:** Nikolaos Mantzaros

**Lyricist:** Dionysios Solomos

**Adopted:** 1823-01-01

adopted 1864; the anthem is based on a 158-stanza poem by the same name, which was inspired by the Greek Revolution of 1821 against the Ottomans (only the first two stanzas are used); Cyprus also uses "Hymn to Freedom" as its anthem
