**泰国** 是一个国家，位于 South-Eastern Asia, Asia，首都是 Bangkok，国歌是 *Thai National Anthem* ("A Salute to the Monarch")。

## 国歌

**英文名称：** A Salute to the Monarch

**作曲：** Phra Chenduriyang (Piti Wathayakon)

**作词：** Luang Saranupraphan

royal anthem, played in the presence of the royal family and during certain state ceremonies
