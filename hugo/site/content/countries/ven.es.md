**Venezuela** es un país en South America, Americas. Su capital es Caracas. El himno nacional es *gloria al bravo pueblo* ("Glory to the Brave People").

## Himno Nacional

**Título en inglés:** Glory to the Brave People

**Compositor:** Lino Gallardo

**Letrista:** Andrés Bello

**Adoptado:** 1810-01-01

adopted 1881; lyrics were written in 1810; both SALIAS and LANDAETA were executed in 1814 during Venezuela's fight for independence
