**Tonga** is a country in Polynesia, Oceania. Its capital is Nuku'alofa. The national anthem is *Ko e fasi 'o e tu'i 'o e 'Otu Tonga* ("Hail to Thee, Land of Our Forefathers").

## National Anthem

**English title:** Hail to Thee, Land of Our Forefathers

**Composer:** Carl Schmitt

adopted 1960, restored 1992; anthem was replaced during one-party rule between 1979 and 1992
