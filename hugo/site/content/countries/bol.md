**Bolivia** is a country in South America, Americas. Its capital is Sucre. The national anthem is *National Anthem of Bolivia* ("Patriotic Song").

## National Anthem

**English title:** Patriotic Song

**Composer:** Leopoldo Benedetto Vincenti

**Lyricist:** José Ignacio de Sanjinés

**Adopted:** 1852
