**Tunisia** is a country in Northern Africa, Africa. Its capital is Tunis. The national anthem is *Humat Al Hima* ("Song of the King of the Tonga Islands").

## National Anthem

**English title:** Song of the King of the Tonga Islands

**Composer:** Mohammed Abdel Wahab

**Lyricist:** Mostafa Saadeq Al-Rafe'ie

in use since 1874; more commonly known as "Fasi Fakafonua" (National Song)
