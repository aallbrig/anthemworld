**Kyrgyzstan** is a country in Central Asia, Asia. Its capital is Bishkek. The national anthem is *State Anthem of the Kyrgyz Republic* ("National Anthem of the Kyrgyz Republic").

## National Anthem

**English title:** National Anthem of the Kyrgyz Republic

**Composer:** Nasyr Davlesov
