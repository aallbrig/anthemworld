**阿尔巴尼亚** 是一个国家，位于 Southeast Europe, Europe，首都是 Tirana，国歌是 *Himni i Flamurit* ("Hymn to the Flag")。

## 国歌

**英文名称：** Hymn to the Flag

**作曲：** Ciprian Ilieș

**作词：** Aleksander Stavre Drenova

adopted 1912; only the first two stanzas of the original poem are used, with the second stanza as a chorus
