**Portugal** es un país en Southern Europe, Europe. Su capital es Lisbon. El himno nacional es *A Portuguesa* ("The Song of the Portuguese").

## Himno Nacional

**Título en inglés:** The Song of the Portuguese

**Compositor:** Alfredo Keil

**Letrista:** Henrique Lopes de Mendonça

adopted 1911; originally written to protest the Portuguese monarchy's acquiescence to the 1890 British ultimatum forcing Portugal to give up areas of Africa
