**Mongolia** is a country in Eastern Asia, Asia. Its capital is Ulan Bator. The national anthem is *National anthem of Mongolia* ("The March of Monaco").

## National Anthem

**English title:** The March of Monaco

**Composer:** Luvsanjambyn Mördorj

**Lyricist:** Tsendiin Damdinsüren

**Adopted:** 1950-01-01

music adopted 1867, lyrics adopted 1931; only the Monegasque lyrics are official; the French version is known as "Hymne Monegasque" (Monegasque Anthem); the words are usually only sung on official occasions
