**Tonga** es un país en Polynesia, Oceania. Su capital es Nuku'alofa. El himno nacional es *Ko e fasi 'o e tu'i 'o e 'Otu Tonga* ("Hail to Thee, Land of Our Forefathers").

## Himno Nacional

**Título en inglés:** Hail to Thee, Land of Our Forefathers

**Compositor:** Carl Schmitt

adopted 1960, restored 1992; anthem was replaced during one-party rule between 1979 and 1992
