**Brazil** is a country in South America, Americas. Its capital is Brasília. The national anthem is *Brazilian National Anthem*.

## National Anthem

**Composer:** Francisco Manuel da Silva

**Lyricist:** Osório Duque-Estrada

music adopted 1890, lyrics adopted 1922; the anthem's music, composed in 1822, was used unofficially for many years
