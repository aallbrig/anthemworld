**斯洛伐克** 是一个国家，位于 Central Europe, Europe，首都是 Bratislava，国歌是 *Nad Tatrou sa blýska* ("Storm Over the Tatras")。

## 国歌

**英文名称：** Storm Over the Tatras

**作词：** Janko Matúška

adopted 1993; music based on an 1843 Slovak folk song "Kopala studienku" (She Was Digging a Well)
