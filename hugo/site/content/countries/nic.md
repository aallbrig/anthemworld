**Nicaragua** is a country in Central America, Americas. Its capital is Managua. The national anthem is *Salve a ti* ("Hail to Thee, Nicaragua").

## National Anthem

**English title:** Hail to Thee, Nicaragua

**Composer:** Luis Abraham Delgadillo

**Lyricist:** Salomón Ibarra Mayorga

music was approved in 1918 and the lyrics in 1939
