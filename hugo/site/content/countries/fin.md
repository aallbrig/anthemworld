**Finland** is a country in Northern Europe, Europe. Its capital is Helsinki. The national anthem is *Maamme* ("Our Land").

## National Anthem

**English title:** Our Land

**Composer:** Fredrik Pacius

**Lyricist:** Johan Ludvig Runeberg

in use since 1848; although never officially adopted, the anthem has been popular since a student group first sang it in 1848
