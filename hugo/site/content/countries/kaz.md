**Kazakhstan** is a country in Central Asia, Asia. Its capital is Astana. The national anthem is *Menıñ Qazaqstanym* ("My Kazakhstan").

## National Anthem

**English title:** My Kazakhstan

**Composer:** Shamshi Kaldayakov

**Lyricist:** Jumeken Najimedenov

adopted 2006; President Nursultan NAZARBAYEV played a role in revising the lyrics
