**Brunei** is a country in South-Eastern Asia, Asia. Its capital is Bandar Seri Begawan. The national anthem is *Allah Peliharakan Sultan* ("God Bless His Majesty").

## National Anthem

**English title:** God Bless His Majesty

**Lyricist:** Yura Halim

**Adopted:** 1951
