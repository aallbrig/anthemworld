**Singapore** es un país en South-Eastern Asia, Asia. Su capital es Singapore. El himno nacional es *Onward Singapore* ("Pluck Your Koras, Strike the Balafons").

## Himno Nacional

**Título en inglés:** Pluck Your Koras, Strike the Balafons

**Compositor:** Zubir Said

**Letrista:** Zubir Said

adopted 1960; lyrics written by Leopold Sedar SENGHOR, Senegal's first president; the anthem sometimes played incorporating the koras (harp-like stringed instruments) and balafons (types of xylophones) mentioned in the title
