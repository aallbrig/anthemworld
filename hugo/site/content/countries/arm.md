**Armenia** is a country in Western Asia, Asia. Its capital is Yerevan. The national anthem is *Mer Hayrenik* ("Our Fatherland").

## National Anthem

**English title:** Our Fatherland

**Composer:** Barsegh Kanachyan

**Lyricist:** Mikael Nalbandian

adopted 1991; based on the anthem of the Democratic Republic of Armenia (1918-1922), but with different lyrics
