**Somalia** is a country in Eastern Africa, Africa. Its capital is Mogadishu. The national anthem is *Qolobaa Calankeed* ("Every Nation Has Its Own Flag").

## National Anthem

**English title:** Every Nation Has Its Own Flag

**Composer:** Abdullahi Qarshe

**Lyricist:** Abdullahi Qarshe
