**Ukraine** is a country in Eastern Europe, Europe. Its capital is Kyiv. The national anthem is *Shche ne vmerla Ukrainy i slava, i volia* ("Ukraine Has Not Yet Perished").

## National Anthem

**English title:** Ukraine Has Not Yet Perished

**Composer:** Mykhailo Verbytskyi

**Lyricist:** Pavlo Chubynskyi

**Adopted:** 2003-03-06

music adopted 1991, lyrics adopted 2003; current version of the anthem is the first verse of CHUBYNSKYI's poem, plus the chorus
