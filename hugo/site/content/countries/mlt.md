**Malta** is a country in Southern Europe, Europe. Its capital is Valletta. The national anthem is *L-Innu Malti* ("The Hymn of Malta").

## National Anthem

**English title:** The Hymn of Malta

**Composer:** Robert Samut

**Lyricist:** Dun Karm Psaila

adopted 1945; written in the form of a prayer to bind together the political parties and the country
