**斯洛文尼亚** 是一个国家，位于 Central Europe, Europe，首都是 Ljubljana，国歌是 *Zdravljica* ("A Toast")。

## 国歌

**英文名称：** A Toast

**作曲：** Stanko Premrl

**作词：** France Prešeren

adopted in 1989; originally written in 1848; only the seventh verse of the poem is used as the anthem
