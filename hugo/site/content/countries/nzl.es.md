**New Zealand** es un país en Australia and New Zealand, Oceania. Su capital es Wellington. El himno nacional es *God Defend New Zealand*.

## Himno Nacional

**Compositor:** John Joseph Woods

**Letrista:** Thomas Henry Smith

**Adoptado:** 1977-01-01

royal anthem and one of two official national anthems; usually played only when a member of the royal family or a representative is present or when allegiance to the crown is demonstrated
