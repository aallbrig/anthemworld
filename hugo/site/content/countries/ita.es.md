**Italy** es un país en Southern Europe, Europe. Su capital es Rome. El himno nacional es *Il Canto degli Italiani* ("The Song of the Italians").

## Himno Nacional

**Título en inglés:** The Song of the Italians

**Compositor:** Michele Novaro

**Letrista:** Goffredo Mameli

**Adoptado:** 1859-01-01

adopted 2005; the anthem, originally written in 1847, is also known as "L'Inno di Mameli" (Mameli's Hymn), and "Fratelli d'Italia" (Brothers of Italy)
