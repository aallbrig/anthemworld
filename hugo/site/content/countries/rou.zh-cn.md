**罗马尼亚** 是一个国家，位于 Southeast Europe, Europe，首都是 Bucharest，国歌是 *Deșteaptă-te, române!* ("Wake up, Romanian!")。

## 国歌

**英文名称：** Wake up, Romanian!

**作曲：** Anton Pann

**作词：** Andrei Mureșanu

adopted 1990; the anthem was written during the 1848 Revolution
