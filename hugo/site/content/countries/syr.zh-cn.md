**叙利亚** 是一个国家，位于 Western Asia, Asia，首都是 Damascus，国歌是 *Humat ad-Diyar* ("Guardians of the Homeland")。

## 国歌

**英文名称：** Guardians of the Homeland

**作曲：** Muhammad Fulayfil

**作词：** Khalil Mardam Bey

adopted 1936, restored 1961; the country had a different anthem between 1958 and 1961, when Syria was part of the United Arab Republic
