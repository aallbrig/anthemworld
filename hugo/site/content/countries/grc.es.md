**Greece** es un país en Southern Europe, Europe. Su capital es Athens. El himno nacional es *Hymn to Liberty* ("Hymn to Freedom").

## Himno Nacional

**Título en inglés:** Hymn to Freedom

**Compositor:** Nikolaos Mantzaros

**Letrista:** Dionysios Solomos

**Adoptado:** 1823-01-01

adopted 1864; the anthem is based on a 158-stanza poem by the same name, which was inspired by the Greek Revolution of 1821 against the Ottomans (only the first two stanzas are used); Cyprus also uses "Hymn to Freedom" as its anthem
