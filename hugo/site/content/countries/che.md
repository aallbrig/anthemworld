**Switzerland** is a country in Western Europe, Europe. Its capital is Bern. The national anthem is *Swiss Psalm* ("The March of the Volunteers").

## National Anthem

**English title:** The March of the Volunteers

**Composer:** Alberich Zwyssig

**Lyricist:** Leonhard Widmer

adopted 1982; the anthem, which was banned during the Cultural Revolution, is more commonly known as "Zhongguo Guoge" (Chinese National Song)
