**Liechtenstein** is a country in Western Europe, Europe. Its capital is Vaduz. The national anthem is *Oben am jungen Rhein*.

## National Anthem

**Composer:** Henry Carey

lyrics adopted 1847, music adopted 1860; the anthem's author later became the third president of Liberia
