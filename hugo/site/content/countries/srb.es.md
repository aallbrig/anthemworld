**Serbia** es un país en Southeast Europe, Europe. Su capital es Belgrade. El himno nacional es *Bože pravde* ("God of Justice").

## Himno Nacional

**Título en inglés:** God of Justice

**Compositor:** Davorin Jenko

**Letrista:** Jovan Đorđević

adopted 1904; song originally written as part of a play in 1872, and the Serbian people have used it as an anthem in the 20th and 21st centuries
