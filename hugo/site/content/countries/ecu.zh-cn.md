**厄瓜多尔** 是一个国家，位于 South America, Americas，首都是 Quito，国歌是 *Salve, Oh Patria* ("We Salute You, Our Homeland")。

## 国歌

**英文名称：** We Salute You, Our Homeland

**作曲：** Antonio Neumane

**作词：** Juan León Mera

adopted 1948; MERA wrote the lyrics in 1865; only the chorus and second verse are sung
