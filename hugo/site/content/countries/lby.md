**Libya** is a country in Northern Africa, Africa. Its capital is Tripoli. The national anthem is *Libya, Libya, Libya*.

## National Anthem

**Composer:** Mohammed Abdel Wahab

**Lyricist:** Bechir Laribi

adopted 1951, but replaced in 1969 when QADHAFI came to power; readopted 2011 with some modification to the lyrics; also known as "Ya Beladi" (O My Country)
