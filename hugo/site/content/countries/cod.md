**DR Congo** is a country in Middle Africa, Africa. Its capital is Kinshasa. The national anthem is *Debout Congolais* ("The Chadian").

## National Anthem

**English title:** The Chadian

**Composer:** Simon-Pierre Boka

**Lyricist:** Joseph Lutumba

**Adopted:** 1960
