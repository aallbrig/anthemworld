**Romania** is a country in Southeast Europe, Europe. Its capital is Bucharest. The national anthem is *Deșteaptă-te, române!* ("Wake up, Romanian!").

## National Anthem

**English title:** Wake up, Romanian!

**Composer:** Anton Pann

**Lyricist:** Andrei Mureșanu

adopted 1990; the anthem was written during the 1848 Revolution
