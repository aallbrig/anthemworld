**Yemen** is a country in Western Asia, Asia. Its capital is Sana'a. The national anthem is *National anthem of Yemen* ("United Republic").

## National Anthem

**English title:** United Republic

**Composer:** Ayoob Tarish

**Lyricist:** Abdullah Abdulwahab Noman

**Adopted:** 1990-01-01

adopted 1990; the music first served as the anthem for South Yemen before unification with North Yemen in 1990
