**Italy** is a country in Southern Europe, Europe. Its capital is Rome. The national anthem is *Il Canto degli Italiani* ("The Song of the Italians").

## National Anthem

**English title:** The Song of the Italians

**Composer:** Michele Novaro

**Lyricist:** Goffredo Mameli

**Adopted:** 1859-01-01

adopted 2005; the anthem, originally written in 1847, is also known as "L'Inno di Mameli" (Mameli's Hymn), and "Fratelli d'Italia" (Brothers of Italy)
