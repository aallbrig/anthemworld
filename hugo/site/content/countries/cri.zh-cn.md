**哥斯达黎加** 是一个国家，位于 Central America, Americas，首都是 San José，国歌是 *Noble patria, tu hermosa bandera* ("National Anthem of Costa Rica")。

## 国歌

**英文名称：** National Anthem of Costa Rica

**作曲：** Manuel María Gutiérrez

**作词：** José María Zeledón Brenes

adopted 1949; the music was originally written for a welcome ceremony in 1852 for the US and UK diplomatic missions; the lyrics were added in 1900
