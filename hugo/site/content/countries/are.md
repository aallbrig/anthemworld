**United Arab Emirates** is a country in Western Asia, Asia. Its capital is Abu Dhabi. The national anthem is *Ishy Bilady* ("National Anthem of the UAE").

## National Anthem

**English title:** National Anthem of the UAE

**Composer:** Mohammed Abdel Wahab

music adopted 1971, lyrics adopted 1986; Mohamad Abdel WAHAB also composed the music for Tunisia's and Libya's anthem
