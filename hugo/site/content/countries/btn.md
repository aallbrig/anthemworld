**Bhutan** is a country in Southern Asia, Asia. Its capital is Thimphu. The national anthem is *Druk Tsenden* ("The Thunder Dragon Kingdom").

## National Anthem

**English title:** The Thunder Dragon Kingdom

**Composer:** Aku Tongmi

**Lyricist:** Dorji Lopen Droep Namgay

**Adopted:** 1953
