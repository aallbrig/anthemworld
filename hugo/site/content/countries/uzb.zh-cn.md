**乌兹别克斯坦** 是一个国家，位于 Central Asia, Asia，首都是 Tashkent，国歌是 *National Anthem of Uzbekistan* ("National Anthem of the Republic of Uzbekistan")。

## 国歌

**英文名称：** National Anthem of the Republic of Uzbekistan

**作曲：** Mutal Burhonov

**作词：** Abdulla Oripov

adopted 1992; after the fall of the Soviet Union, Uzbekistan kept the music of its Soviet-era anthem but adopted new lyrics
