**Norway** is a country in Northern Europe, Europe. Its capital is Oslo. The national anthem is *Ja, vi elsker dette landet* ("Song of the King").

## National Anthem

**English title:** Song of the King

**Composer:** Rikard Nordraak

**Lyricist:** Bjørnstjerne Bjørnson
