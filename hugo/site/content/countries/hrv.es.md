**Croatia** es un país en Southeast Europe, Europe. Su capital es Zagreb. El himno nacional es *Lijepa naša domovino* ("Our Beautiful Homeland").

## Himno Nacional

**Título en inglés:** Our Beautiful Homeland

**Compositor:** Josip Runjanin

**Letrista:** Antun Mihanović

adopted in 1972 while still part of Yugoslavia; the lyrics were written in 1835, and it served as an unofficial anthem beginning in 1891
