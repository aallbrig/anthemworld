**Malawi** is a country in Eastern Africa, Africa. Its capital is Lilongwe. The national anthem is *Mulungu dalitsa Malaŵi* ("O God, Bless Our Land of Malawi").

## National Anthem

**English title:** O God, Bless Our Land of Malawi

**Composer:** Michael-Fredrick Paul Sauka

**Lyricist:** Michael-Fredrick Paul Sauka

**Adopted:** 1964
