**Philippines** is a country in South-Eastern Asia, Asia. Its capital is Manila. The national anthem is *Lupang Hinirang* ("Chosen Land").

## National Anthem

**English title:** Chosen Land

**Composer:** Julián Felipe

**Lyricist:** José Palma

music adopted 1898 and lyrics adopted 1956; only sung in Tagalog
