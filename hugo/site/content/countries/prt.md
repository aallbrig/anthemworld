**Portugal** is a country in Southern Europe, Europe. Its capital is Lisbon. The national anthem is *A Portuguesa* ("The Song of the Portuguese").

## National Anthem

**English title:** The Song of the Portuguese

**Composer:** Alfredo Keil

**Lyricist:** Henrique Lopes de Mendonça

adopted 1911; originally written to protest the Portuguese monarchy's acquiescence to the 1890 British ultimatum forcing Portugal to give up areas of Africa
