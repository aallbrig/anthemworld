**New Zealand** is a country in Australia and New Zealand, Oceania. Its capital is Wellington. The national anthem is *God Defend New Zealand*.

## National Anthem

**Composer:** John Joseph Woods

**Lyricist:** Thomas Henry Smith

**Adopted:** 1977-01-01

royal anthem and one of two official national anthems; usually played only when a member of the royal family or a representative is present or when allegiance to the crown is demonstrated
