**Belgium** is a country in Western Europe, Europe. Its capital is Brussels. The national anthem is *The Brabançonne* ("The Song of Brabant").

## National Anthem

**English title:** The Song of Brabant

**Composer:** François van Campenhout

**Lyricist:** Alexandre Dechet

adopted 1830; according to legend Louis-Alexandre DECHET, an actor at the theater where the revolution against the Netherlands began, wrote the lyrics with a group of young people in a Brussels cafe
