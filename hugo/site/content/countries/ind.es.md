**India** es un país en Southern Asia, Asia. Su capital es New Delhi. El himno nacional es *Jana Gana Mana* ("Thou Art the Ruler of the Minds of All People").

## Himno Nacional

**Título en inglés:** Thou Art the Ruler of the Minds of All People

**Compositor:** Margaret Cousins

**Letrista:** Rabindranath Tagore

adopted 1950; Rabindranath TAGORE, a Nobel laureate, also wrote Bangladesh's national anthem
