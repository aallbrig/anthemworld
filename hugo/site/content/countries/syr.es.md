**Syria** es un país en Western Asia, Asia. Su capital es Damascus. El himno nacional es *Humat ad-Diyar* ("Guardians of the Homeland").

## Himno Nacional

**Título en inglés:** Guardians of the Homeland

**Compositor:** Muhammad Fulayfil

**Letrista:** Khalil Mardam Bey

adopted 1936, restored 1961; the country had a different anthem between 1958 and 1961, when Syria was part of the United Arab Republic
