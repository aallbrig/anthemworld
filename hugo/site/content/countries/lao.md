**Laos** is a country in South-Eastern Asia, Asia. Its capital is Vientiane. The national anthem is *Pheng Xat Lao* ("Hymn of the Lao People").

## National Anthem

**English title:** Hymn of the Lao People

**Composer:** Thongdy Sounthone Vichit

**Lyricist:** Sisana Sisane

music adopted 1945, lyrics adopted 1975; the anthem's lyrics were changed after the communist revolution that overthrew the monarchy in 1975
