**North Macedonia** is a country in Southeast Europe, Europe. Its capital is Skopje. The national anthem is *Denes nad Makedonija* ("Today Over Macedonia").

## National Anthem

**English title:** Today Over Macedonia

**Composer:** Todor Skalovski

**Lyricist:** Vlado Maleski

written in 1943 and adopted in 1991, the song previously served as the anthem of the Socialist Republic of Macedonia, when it was part of Yugoslavia
